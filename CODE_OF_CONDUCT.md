# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and specific. Critique the change, not the person.
- Assume good faith. People arrive with different tools, constraints and levels of experience.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no publishing of other people's private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through GitHub's private reporting on this repository (Security tab → Report a vulnerability, and say it is a conduct report), or by contacting [@stLalo](https://github.com/stLalo) directly on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block accounts that break these rules, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
